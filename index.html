Migraine vs. Headache: Helping OTC Buyers Find the Right Relief

Pharma SEO Branded
Goal
Drive OTC product consideration
Audience
UAE adults, self-medicating
Format
Long-form branded blog
Target Keywords
Migraine vs headache, ibuprofen 400mg
Is it just a headache or something more? When a sudden pounding starts and stops you in your tracks, it's natural to wonder. Understanding the difference is key to finding fast, effective relief — especially when your day can't slow down.
Approached clinical differentiation between migraine and headache in patient-friendly language, then introduced ibuprofen 400mg as the evidence-backed OTC solution — backed by Cochrane-level data and framed around the reader's real-world experience rather than a product claim.
Case Study 02
Selecting the Right Silicone Scar Sheet — Turning Clinical Evidence into a Buyer Decision
Medical Device Brand · UAE · Buyer's Guide + Patient Education · English
Medical Device Wound Care Product
Goal
Educate & convert post-surgical patients
Audience
Post-op patients, UAE
Format
Buyer's guide + step-by-step guide
Challenge
High anxiety, low product awareness
You didn't go through surgery just to be left with a scar you want to hide. Medical-grade silicone scar sheets are the gold standard in modern scar care — clinically proven for both new and existing scars. You don't have to wait and see.
Addressed emotional purchase context first (post-surgical anxiety), then established clinical credibility for silicone sheeting, before guiding readers through a practical selection framework — designed to increase product confidence and reduce abandonment at the consideration stage.
Case Study 03
Medical Centre vs Hospital — Reducing ER Overcrowding Through Patient Decision Content
UAE Healthcare Client · SEO Blog Article · Patient Decision · English
Healthcare UAE SEO
Goal
Redirect patients from ER to medical centres
Audience
UAE residents, general public
Format
SEO blog, patient-facing
Key Message
Right care, right place, right time
The difference between a medical centre and a hospital isn't about quality of care — it's about the nature of the condition. For issues that don't require admission or major surgery, a well-equipped medical centre is often the fastest, most appropriate option.
Reframed the patient's default assumption (hospital = better) through reassurance and practical comparison, incorporating the brand's continuity-of-care narrative (unified electronic records) as a trust signal rather than a product feature.
